To Do MCP: opening the app the first time
==========================================

To Do MCP is free and open source, but it isn't signed by Apple (that needs a paid Apple
developer account). So the first time you open it, macOS blocks it with a message like
"Apple could not verify 'To Do MCP' is free of malware". Only allow it if you downloaded it
from https://github.com/QingyaoAi/Microsoft-To-do-MCP/releases

1. Drag "To Do MCP" onto the Applications shortcut in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "To Do MCP".
   When macOS says it can't be opened, click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about "To Do MCP". Confirm with your password or
   Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" once more.

4. A checklist icon appears in the menu bar. Click it and choose "Sign In..." to connect
   your Microsoft account, then "Connect to Agent" for your AI tools.

You only need to do this once.

Terminal alternative to steps 2-3 (removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag):

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/To Do MCP.app"

Please run it from Applications. If you open it straight from this disk image or from
Downloads, macOS runs it from a temporary location, and agents you connect would lose track
of it later.

Requirements: macOS 13 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer).
Source code, help and the MIT license: https://github.com/QingyaoAi/Microsoft-To-do-MCP
